What happens technically when you visit

This is a static website served from third-party servers which, like any internet server, automatically log the IP address and technical details of the connection in order to deliver the pages and detect abuse. We do not combine that information with anything else and we do not use it to profile you.

  • Hosting: GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) or Vercel Inc., depending on the site, acting as processors.
  • Typefaces: some pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which transmits your IP address to Google. It sets no cookies.

This website also embeds third-party content that can set cookies in your browser. It stays blocked until you accept it, and the cookie policy explains how.

This website uses Google Analytics, from Google Ireland Limited, to obtain statistics about its visits, with your consent as the legal basis. It is not loaded until you accept it, it does not receive what you type into forms, and its personalised advertising signals are turned off. Google may process this data in the United States, with the safeguards required by European law. The cookie policy explains how.
