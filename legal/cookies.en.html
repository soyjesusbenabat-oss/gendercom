Cookie policy

This website uses third-party tools that set cookies to measure its visits and results. We keep them blocked until you decide. This page explains what each item is and how to change your mind.

What a cookie is

A cookie is a small file that a website stores in your browser to remember something: that you have already signed in, which language you prefer or, in the case of advertising, which pages you have visited. The law only requires permission for those that are not strictly necessary.

Third-party cookies on this website

Any cookies that appear come from these third-party tools:

  • Google Analytics (Google Ireland Limited): gives us statistics on how many people visit this website, which pages they view, where they come from and what they do there, such as clicking a registration link, downloading a document. It sets the cookies _ga and _ga_*, on this domain or on its parent domain, which last up to two years and tell one browser from another but do not tell us who you are. If you decline or withdraw your permission, we delete them from both places. Google keeps detailed events for 2 months and data linked to each browser for up to 14 months from the last visit; after that only aggregate figures remain.
  • Venue map: the venue section embeds a Google Maps map. Loading it allows Google to set cookies in your browser and to know that you have visited this page. They are Google's cookies, not ours.
  • External form: some editions embed a Tally form (Tally BV, Belgium), which sets technical cookies so that the form works and remembers what you have typed.

None of this loads when you open the website. It stays blocked until you allow it through the notice shown on your first visit. If you decline, the website works just the same: your visit is not measured and you will simply see a box in place of the embedded content, with a link to open it at source.

Not cookies, but worth knowing

Some pages load typefaces from the Google Fonts servers. That sets no cookies, but it does transmit your IP address to Google so that the font file can be sent to you. The same applies to images and icons served from content delivery networks.

The server hosting this website automatically logs the requests it receives, with the IP address and the time, in order to deliver the pages and detect abuse. That is a technical log, not a cookie, and it is not used to profile you.

How to change your mind

You can change your choice at any time from the «Cookies» link in the footer of this website, which brings the notice back.

If you withdraw your permission, the measurement tags stop working and we delete from your browser any cookies they had set on this domain or on its parent domain.

Every browser lets you see, delete and block the cookies that have been set. You will find the option in the privacy settings of Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge or Opera. Blocking third-party cookies does not affect how this website works.

The conference platform

If you register or submit a proposal you will leave this website and enter the platform (conferences.portalintracom.com), which uses its own technical cookies to keep your session open. They are explained in its own policy: https://conferences.portalintracom.com/en/legal/cookies.
